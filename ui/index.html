Propnight is still running

Closing the launcher does not close the game, you can keep playing. But until you open the launcher again:

  • your progress (coins, skins, rank) is not saved online: it is uploaded the next time you open the launcher,
  • your friends see you offline and their invites will not reach you in the game,
  • your match results and player reports wait on your PC and are sent later.
